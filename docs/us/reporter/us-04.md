
## US-04: Receive Notifications About Reported Issues

**As a** reporter (student or university staff member),  
**I want to** receive notifications when the status of my reported issues changes,  
**So that** I can stay informed about their progress.

**Related Requirements:** FR-22

### Acceptance Criteria

**AC 1: Receive a notification after a status change**

Given the reporter has submitted a report  
When the status of its associated issue changes  
Then the system shall generate a notification for the reporter  
And the notification shall identify the relevant issue  
And indicate its new status.

**AC 2: Receive notifications for multiple status changes**

Given the reporter has submitted a report  
When the associated issue undergoes multiple status changes  
Then the system shall generate a notification for each status change.

**AC 3: Access an issue from its notification**

Given the reporter has received a notification about a status change  
When the reporter selects the notification  
Then the system shall display the corresponding report or issue details.
