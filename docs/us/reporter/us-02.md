
## US-02: View Submitted Reports

**As a** reporter (student or university staff member),  
**I want to** view the reports I have submitted,  
**So that** I can access and keep track of the issues I have reported.

**Related Requirements:** FR-16

### Acceptance Criteria

**AC 1: View submitted reports**

Given the reporter is authenticated  
And the reporter has submitted one or more reports  
When the reporter opens the submitted reports section  
Then the system shall display the reports submitted by that reporter.

**AC 2: View report details**

Given the reporter has submitted at least one report  
When the reporter selects one of their reports  
Then the system shall display the details of that report, including its description, category, location, and attached images when available.

**AC 3: View an empty report list**

Given the reporter has not submitted any reports  
When the reporter opens the submitted reports section  
Then the system shall display a message indicating that no reports are available.

**AC 4: Prevent access to another user's reports**

Given the reporter is authenticated  
And another user has submitted a report  
When the reporter attempts to access that report without authorization  
Then the system shall deny access to the report.
