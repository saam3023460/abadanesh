# Abadanesh

## About the Project

**Abadanesh** is a university issue management platform designed to help resolve problems reported by students and university staff.

The system provides a platform for reporting and managing issues related to university infrastructure, electrical systems, technical equipment, sanitation, campus facilities, and other university services. It also allows users to submit suggestions for improving the university environment.

Abadanesh focuses on the entire problem-solving process, enabling administrators to review reports, assign them to responsible departments, monitor their progress, and follow up until the issues are resolved.
